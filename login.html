Platform Overview

Integrated e-commerce ecosystem

The ONRUF e-commerce platform is an integrated digital system that streamlines online buying and selling across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with the flexibility to scale into new markets. Alongside commerce features, it equips teams with the administrative tools needed to support and monitor daily operations from a single hub.

  • Dedicated interfaces for individual accounts, business accounts, and a central control panel.
  • Safe environment for browsing products, completing sales, and joining auctions or negotiated offers.
  • Web and mobile access with bilingual Arabic and English support and integrated electronic payments.
  • Commerce essentials including favorites, shopping carts, notifications, seller & product ratings, and shipment tracking.

Designed to strengthen trust between shoppers and sellers throughout the ONRUF network
